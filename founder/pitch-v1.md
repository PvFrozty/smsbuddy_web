SMS Buddy: business SMS for Sri Lanka, at smsbuddy.lk.

Running an 11.11 sale? Use SMS Buddy to announce it to your whole customer list, remind them on the day, and confirm orders and deliveries, in Sinhala, Tamil or English. Messages are sent under your business name once that name is approved after signup.

Price: pay as you go at LKR 0.58 per SMS, or buy a package: LKR 560 for 1,000 SMS (0.56 each) up to LKR 45,000 for 100,000 SMS (0.45 each). Prepaid credits, valid for 3 months, no monthly subscription. Long or Sinhala/Tamil messages can use more than one credit.

Create a free account at portal.smsbuddy.lk/register, then top up when you're ready to send.
