# SMS Buddy

- What it is: a prepaid business SMS service for Sri Lanka (smsbuddy.lk). Businesses send bulk SMS under their own approved business name (sender ID), in Sinhala, Tamil or English, from a web portal or an API, with delivery reports.
- Who it is for: owners and marketers of Sri Lankan small and medium businesses that run seasonal sales and already have a customer phone list (social-commerce sellers, shops, restaurants, online stores); marketing agencies second.
- What it sells, at what price: SMS credits in packages from LKR 560 for 1,000 SMS (0.56 each) to LKR 45,000 for 100,000 SMS (0.45 each). Prepaid, credits never expire (confirmed 10 November), no monthly subscription. Pay as you go (LKR 0.58) and billing after sending are reserved for customers of other Nanobotz services and are not advertised.
- Where and how: online across Sri Lanka. Sign up free at portal.smsbuddy.lk/register, request a sender name (approved after signup), top up, send. Run by Nanobotz IT Solutions. Customers find it through the website, search, and a planned 11.11 (11 November) and 12.12 ad campaign on Meta and Google.
- Budget and constraints: ad budget not set. Sender-name approval time not confirmed. No substantiated customer results, testimonials or delivery statistics to use in marketing. 11.11 is one day away (today is 10 November 2026).
