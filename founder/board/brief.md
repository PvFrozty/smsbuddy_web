# SMS Buddy

- What it is: a prepaid business SMS service for Sri Lanka (smsbuddy.lk). Businesses send bulk SMS under their own approved business name (sender ID), in Sinhala, Tamil or English, from a web portal or an API, with delivery reports.
- Who it is for: owners and marketers of Sri Lankan small and medium businesses that run seasonal sales and already have a customer phone list (social-commerce sellers, shops, restaurants, online stores); marketing agencies second.
- What it sells, at what price: SMS credits. Pay as you go LKR 0.58 per SMS, or packages from LKR 560 for 1,000 SMS (0.56 each) to LKR 45,000 for 100,000 SMS (0.45 each). Prepaid, credits valid 3 months, no monthly subscription. Nanobotz clients paying over LKR 50,000 a month can be billed after sending, at a rate set from their volume.
- Where and how: online across Sri Lanka. Sign up free at portal.smsbuddy.lk/register, request a sender name (approved after signup), top up, send. Run by Nanobotz IT Solutions. Customers find it through the website, search, and a planned 11.11 (11 November) and 12.12 ad campaign on Meta and Google.
- Budget and constraints: ad budget not set. Sender-name approval time not confirmed. No substantiated customer results, testimonials or delivery statistics to use in marketing. 11.11 is one day away (today is 10 November 2026).

## Facts a stranger needs

**Competitors** (their own public pages, checked 9 September 2026; not purchased):
- Notify.lk: pay-and-go at LKR 7,500 one-off, LKR 0.60/SMS before tax, no monthly fee, five sender IDs, 6-month credit validity; free trial credits with no card; extra charge for promotional SMS; shows customer quotes and integrations.
- Text.lk: no setup or monthly fee on Starter, tax-inclusive rates from LKR 0.84 down to 0.64, credits never expire; a LKR 549/month Business plan; ten free trial units and a demo sender ID; custom sender-ID approval usually hours to one business day, sometimes three.
- SMSGo.lk: LKR 0.54/SMS including tax, no setup or monthly fee, credits never expire, Unicode, REST API, a WooCommerce plugin; brand sender names need approval.
- The free alternative most small sellers use: WhatsApp broadcasts and groups, Facebook and Instagram posts.

**What SMS Buddy has today:** a website with a pricing page, package table and calculator; a portal with signup and email verification; branded sender IDs, shared credits across brands, bulk sending, API, delivery reports, assisted setup (all claims from its own site; backend not independently verified). No free trial credits. Credits expire after 3 months (competitors: 6 months or never). Sinhala and Tamil messages may use more than one credit; the exact rule isn't published.

**Simulated buyer panel** (20 AI-played buyers from the target profile, shown the 11.11 pitch; good for finding objections, not for predicting conversion):
- 1 buy, 19 pass. The one yes was a cautious shop owner buying the LKR 560 pack as a test.
- Price was not the objection: median "bargain" at LKR 0.40/SMS, "getting expensive" at 0.75, "too expensive" about 1.10.
- Passes: habit (12: WhatsApp is free, or they already have an approved sender name elsewhere); the two-day window (sign up, wait for approval, upload a list: too much before the sale); "Sinhala uses more credits" read as a hidden price; no way to test before paying; one owner unsure SMS promotions are legal without an opt-out.
- What would flip a no: free test SMS at signup to their own phone under their own name (7 buyers); a stated approval time such as within one working day (6); the real cost of a normal Sinhala message shown up front (5); done-for-you setup over WhatsApp (4); proof SMS beats WhatsApp for sales (4); a WooCommerce/POS plugin or API with setup help (2).
