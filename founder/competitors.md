## Price range, entry pay-as-you-go rate per SMS

- **Lowest:** LKR 0.54 (SMSGo, tax included).
- **Median:** about LKR 0.64 across the nine rivals that publish a rate.
- **Highest:** LKR 1.00 (LKHoster, TXTMSG's smallest plan).

SMS Buddy's public entry point is SB1X: 1,000 SMS for LKR 560 (0.56), tax included (owner confirmed 10 November). That is below the median, 2 cents above SMSGo's 0.54 (which also sells 1,000 SMS for LKR 540) and 3 cents below SMSlenz. Pay as you go (0.58) is reserved for Nanobotz service customers and isn't advertised. Say "tax included" on the site and in ads, as rivals do.

At 100,000 SMS, SMS Buddy's 0.45 (tax included) is the lowest published package rate.

## What their customers complain about

Public reviews are thin, so every theme below is **thin** (fewer than three reviews):

- **Approval and support take time** (Notify.lk). One 5-star Trustpilot review, posted twice under different names in June 2024: "It takes more time for approval and customer support, but overall, the service is good." ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/notify.lk))
- **Cost per SMS, and monthly plans that don't roll into top-up credit** (Notify.lk). A 2-star review from December 2022: "1 cost per sms can be improved." ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/notify.lk))
- **Nothing else to go on:**
  - Text.lk: 11 Trustpilot reviews, all 5-star.
  - Notify's WooCommerce plugin: 3 reviews, all 5-star.
  - Dialog's e-SMS WordPress plugin: no reviews, and its page says it may no longer be maintained.
  - oZoneSender on Shopify: no reviews.

So public complaints don't reveal an obvious weakness to attack. The objections in the buyer panel (`founder/panel/results.md`) remain the better guide, though they are simulated.

## The gap

**There is no gap on product terms, and SMS Buddy is behind on several.** What the board asked SMS Buddy to add already exists at rivals:

| The board's condition | Already offered by |
|---|---|
| Free test credits at signup | SMSGo, SMSlenz, Text.lk, Notify, FITSMS, DigitalNext, TXTMSG |
| A demo sender name while yours is approved | SMSlenz, Text.lk |
| A stated approval time | SMSGo (24 hours), SMSlenz (1 business day) |
| Sinhala/Tamil priced clearly | SMSlenz (same rate as English); FITSMS publishes 70 characters per SMS |
| Credits that last | never expire at SMSGo, SMSlenz, Text.lk, FITSMS, SMSAPI; 6 months at Notify |
| WooCommerce / Shopify plugins | five rivals |

**Matching these is necessary, but it only buys a place at the table.** Three openings remain, tied to evidence:

1. **Nobody sells the sale itself.** Every rival positions on rate, API and "trusted gateway". None packages a done-for-you seasonal campaign: list loaded over WhatsApp, the first message written in Sinhala, Tamil or English, a reminder on the day, order confirmations. The panel asked for exactly this (4 of 20), as did last-minute planners. It's service, not software, so it's harder for a gateway to copy, but it costs staff time.
2. **The cheapest published 100k rate (0.45).** This is a real edge for high-volume senders, and it includes tax. No rival advertises lower at that volume except Notify's 0.40 overage, which sits behind an LKR 18,000 monthly plan and excludes tax.
3. **Nanobotz's own clients, billed after sending.** No rival has this base, or this billing. It's the one channel where SMS Buddy doesn't have to win a comparison table.

## The threat

**SMSGo** could copy SMS Buddy fastest. It already holds the cheap-and-easy corner (0.54 with tax, 24-hour approval, plugins, credits that never expire). It would only need a "sale campaign" page to take the seasonal angle too. **SMSlenz** is close behind: it already gives a demo sender and charges Unicode at the English rate.

The free option, WhatsApp, needs no copying: 12 of the 20 panel buyers stayed with it out of habit.

## What this means for the 11.11 campaign

- **Don't run price-led ads to small sellers.** SMSGo is cheaper at entry (0.54 against 0.56). Credits now never expire at SMS Buddy either, so that's no longer a gap.
- **The 11.11 ad is safe as it stands.** It sells the sale moment, which no rival does, and makes no price claim.
- **Before scaling spend, fix the terms that lose comparisons:**
  1. Say "tax included" next to every price (confirmed; the site still tells buyers to check).
  2. Say on the site and in ads that every account gets 10 free test SMS (it already exists; rivals advertise theirs).
  3. Publish an approval time.
  4. Publish the Sinhala credit rule.
  5. ~~Reconsider the 3-month expiry~~ Done: the owner confirmed on 10 November that credits never expire. Say so everywhere.
