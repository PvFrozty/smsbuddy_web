## What would flip a no

- A guaranteed sender-name approval time (like within 24 hours) plus a few hundred free test credits so I can check delivery to Dialog, Mobitel and Hutch numbers before I bill a client for it.
- A clear per-message cost for a typical Sinhala offer plus a guarantee my business name is approved within a day, so I could test it on one branch's list before the sale.
- Someone from SMS Buddy setting it up for me on WhatsApp the same day: I send them my list as it is, they load it and the first message goes out under my shop name before the sale.
- A free trial batch of a few hundred SMS under my own business name, with delivery and click stats, plus a clear rate that beats the other two providers I compare.
- Agency features: one account managing several client sender IDs with separate credit, plus proof of delivery reports and a guaranteed sender ID approval time. Then I'd test it on one smaller client.
- A clear table showing how many credits one normal Sinhala sale message uses, with the real cost per message, that comes out clearly cheaper than my current provider, and my sender name approved within a day.
- A few free test SMS on signup so I can send one to my own phone under my shop name and see it arrive before I put any money in.
- If whoever runs my marketing showed me that one SMS blast for a sale brought in clearly more customers or orders than our WhatsApp posts did, with the cost worked out for my list size.
- A clearly lower rate at my volume, say under 0.40 per SMS, plus them importing my list and getting my sender name live for me within a day, with a small test send first.
- A clear WooCommerce/POS plugin or API with someone who sets it up for me and imports my Excel list, so confirmations go out automatically from day one.
- Seeing proof that an SMS blast actually brings back customers who ignore or have muted my WhatsApp broadcasts, maybe with a small free trial batch so I can test it on my own list before topping up.
- A clear per-message price for a normal Sinhala message, shown up front, that beats the other providers, plus a few free test messages after signup.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
