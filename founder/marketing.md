# SMS Buddy: 11.11 campaign (9 November to 12 December 2026)

Goal: new SMS Buddy accounts at portal.smsbuddy.lk/register, then a first top-up.

**Evidence used.**
- A 20-buyer simulated panel (`founder/panel/results.md`, seed 330632) was shown the 11.11 pitch in `founder/pitch.md`.
- The competitor research in `docs/conversion-research.md` (Notify.lk, Text.lk and SMSGo.lk, checked 9 September 2026).
- The November 2026 price list.

The buyer profile in `founder/customer.json` is a written guess, and the panel is simulated: it is good at finding objections and bad at predicting real conversion. Treat the audience calls as hypotheses to test with the first week of ad results.

## What the buyer panel said

**1 buy, 19 pass (5%).** The only yes was a cautious shop owner buying the LKR 560 pack as a test.

**Price is not the problem.** Median answers in LKR per SMS: a bargain at 0.40, getting expensive at 0.75, too expensive at about 1.10. SMS Buddy's public rates (0.56 to 0.45) sit inside that range.

**Why they passed:**
- **Habit (12 of 19).** WhatsApp broadcasts are free, or they already have an approved sender name with another provider.
- **The two-day window.** Sign up, wait for approval, upload a list: too much before 11.11. This confirms the "be ready for your next sale" angle.
- **"Sinhala costs more credits" backfired.** Five buyers read it as "0.58 isn't my real price" and wanted to compare Sinhala costs.
- **Can't try it first.** Several wouldn't pay before seeing one SMS arrive under their own name.

**What would flip a no** (count of buyers asking for it):

| Ask | Buyers | Who decides |
|---|---:|---|
| A few free test SMS at signup, to own phone, under own name | 7 | You: a business decision |
| A stated sender-name approval time ("within 1 working day") | 6 | You: only if it's true |
| The real cost of a normal Sinhala message, shown up front | 5 | Confirm how SMS Buddy counts Unicode, then publish it |
| Done-for-you setup: send your list on WhatsApp, we load it | 4 | You: a service decision |
| Proof SMS reaches people who mute WhatsApp | 4 | Needs real results; can't be faked in ads |
| WooCommerce or POS plugin, or API with setup help | 2 | Product |

**What this changes in the plan:**
1. **Lead with "try it first", not price.** If you can give free test credits on signup, it is the single biggest lever in the panel. Without them, push the LKR 560 pack as the test.
2. **Never mention Sinhala without its cost.** Phones usually fit 70 Sinhala or Tamil characters in one SMS, against 160 in English. Confirm SMS Buddy counts it this way, then say "A Sinhala message up to 70 characters is 1 credit."
3. **Answer approval time in the ad, if you can stand behind a number.**
4. **Offer setup over WhatsApp.** "Send us your list, we'll set you up" answers the last-minute planners and the people who hand marketing to someone else. Both groups said no.
5. **Answer the legal question once.** One buyer wouldn't approve SMS promotions without knowing it's allowed and that customers can opt out. Add an opt-out line to every message, and an FAQ on the site.
6. **Agencies need multi-brand sender names and delivery reports.** Keep them secondary until those are in the ads.

## The problem this campaign has to work around

A sender name has to be approved after signup. Text.lk says its approvals take hours to a few days; SMS Buddy's own timing isn't confirmed. A business that signs up on 10 November may not be sending as its own name by 11.11.

So the campaign uses 11.11 as the hook, but sells **being ready for the next sale**: Black Friday (28 November) and 12.12. That is honest, and it gives the ads a three-week life instead of two days.

## Positioning

1. **Recommended.** For Sri Lankan shops and online sellers who run sales but can't reach every customer, SMS Buddy is business SMS that sends your offer to your whole list under your shop's name, in Sinhala, Tamil or English, unlike a WhatsApp group most customers have muted.
   *Why:* every competitor sells on price, API and features. None leads with the sale moment. WhatsApp, not another SMS provider, is the real alternative for small sellers.
2. For businesses already sending SMS, SMS Buddy is prepaid SMS from LKR 0.45 with no monthly fee, unlike plans that charge every month.
   *Weak:* SMSGo lists LKR 0.54 tax-inclusive and Text.lk's credits never expire, so price alone doesn't win. Use it only for the switcher audience.
3. For agencies running seasonal campaigns for several brands, SMS Buddy is one account with credits shared across brands.
   *Secondary audience; small budget.*

## Ten hooks

Each hook answers one objection from `founder/customer.json`. Format: the first 3 seconds of a video or the ad headline.

| # | Hook | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | See it arrive on your phone, under your shop name, before you pay. (Confirmed 10 November: every new account gets 10 free SMS. Lead with this.) | Can't try it first. Panel's top ask |
| 1 | Daraz is shouting about 11.11. Is your shop? (Panel warned SMS can get lost among big-brand blasts, so pair it with the shop-name hook.) | Relevance this week |
| 2 | Not every customer is in your WhatsApp group. Every customer has SMS. | "WhatsApp is free" |
| 3 | Your shop's name on every message, not a random number. | "Will they think it's spam?" |
| 4 | Get your business name approved now. Be ready for 12.12. | Approval timing |
| 5 | One message. Every customer. Down to LKR 0.45 each. (Pay as you go is not advertised: it is reserved for Nanobotz service customers.) | "What will I pay?" |
| 6 | Start with LKR 560 for 1,000 SMS. | Cautious first-timer |
| 7 | No monthly fee. Top up when you have a sale. | "I don't send that often" |
| 8 | Write it in Sinhala, Tamil or English. A Sinhala message up to 70 characters is 1 credit. (Use only once the credit rule is confirmed; without the second sentence the panel read it as hidden cost.) | Language and real cost |
| 11 | Send us your customer list on WhatsApp. We'll set you up. (Only if the team can do this.) | Setup, last-minute planners |
| 9 | Order confirmed. Out for delivery. Your customers know. | Order and delivery updates |
| 10 | Running sales for five brands? One account, shared credits. | Agencies |

Always state beside any price that credits never expire and that long or Sinhala/Tamil messages can use more than one credit. Never say "cheapest" (SMSGo lists a lower rate) or give read rates (no evidence).

## Budget

No budget was given. The split, whatever the total:

- Meta ads: 60% (of which 15% goes to retargeting from 24 November).
- Google Search: 30%.
- Re-editing creative: 10% (a 12.12 end card, a Sinhala-language version of the ad).

**The most you can pay for a new account.** Gross profit from a customer's first three months × the share of accounts that top up. Example arithmetic only: if a typical new customer tops up an SB5X (LKR 2,700) and you keep G% of it, and 1 in 3 accounts tops up, the ceiling is 2,700 × G% ÷ 3. Your cost and margin sheet has G%. That figure is internal and should never appear in ads or on the site.

## Three numbers to watch weekly

1. **Cost per account created** (Meta and Google, separately). Change creative if it's above the ceiling for 3 days running.
2. **Accounts that top up within 14 days.** Below 20% means signups aren't the problem: fix onboarding and how long sender-name approval takes.
3. **Time from signup to approved sender name.** Above 2 working days, stop running "be ready for 12.12" after 5 December.
