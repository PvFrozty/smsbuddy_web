# SMS Buddy video campaign brief

Prepared 9 September 2026 from `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/conversion-research.md`. Creative proposal; no campaign has been published or funded.

## Objective and audience

Help Sri Lankan small-business owners understand branded SMS and choose **Get Started → create an account**. Completed accounts are the outcome; video views and signup clicks are supporting metrics. Audience priority is a working hypothesis.

## Three-scene treatment, 15 seconds

| Time | Main copy | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Bring customers back. | Original generated photograph of small-business packaging hands; introduce SMS Buddy early. |
| 4–10s | Your brand. Their inbox. | Composed recipient-message mockup with a clearly visible **Example message** label. |
| 10–15s | Get Started / Create your account | SMS Buddy end card; **Your customers are a message away.** and **smsbuddy.lk**. |

Keep the example free of real personal data, customer logos, delivery statistics and testimonial styling. The finished example reads: **New arrivals are here. Visit us this weekend.** Its fictional sender is **YOUR STORE**. Do not show sent/delivered confirmations as actual product evidence.

## Ad copy

**Primary text:** Keep customers in the loop with branded business SMS. Promotions, reminders and updates for businesses in Sri Lanka.

**Headline:** Business SMS for Sri Lanka

**Suggested platform CTA:** Sign Up, if available for the selected objective. The video retains Get Started to match the website.

**Registration destination:** [Create an SMS Buddy account](https://portal.smsbuddy.lk/register), as recorded in `PRODUCT.md`. Registration and production activation are separate steps.

## Export and placement plan

| Version | Intended use | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| 16:9 landscape | Website video | Separate landscape layout; preserve readable message example and CTA. |
| 9:16 portrait | Meta Reels / Stories creative | Place every essential word, brand mark and example label in a central safe region. |
| 4:5 portrait | Meta feed creative | Recompose for feed; keep essential text comfortably clear of edges. |

Meta's official [Reels ads overview](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) recommends vertical 9:16 creative, audio and keeping key messages inside the safe zone; it links a safe-zone checker. Its search-indexed official content was available during this review, but direct opening redirected to login/temporary blocking. Its performance-study percentages are not predictions for SMS Buddy and must not appear in campaign claims.

For production, use a **conservative working inset of 14% at the top, 40% at the bottom and 6% on either side of the 9:16 canvas** for all essential elements. At 1080×1920 this is approximately **x=65–1015, y=269–1152**. These dimensions are a design constraint chosen for this project, not a claim that current placement specifications were fully verified. Decorative imagery can extend beyond it. Give essential text more room at the right edge where interface controls appear.

Final placement previews should be checked against Meta's [Instagram Reels video guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels) and [Facebook Feed video guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/facebook-feed). Both direct pages were login/temporarily blocked during this review, so current exact codec, resolution, duration and placement acceptance limits remain unverified. The 15-second duration and 4:5 feed composition are campaign choices. Use separate placement assets and inspect actual previews rather than assuming automatic cropping preserves the message.

Make the story fully understandable with sound off. Music or sound design can support transitions, with rights confirmed for paid advertising. Keep actual copy in the composition rather than relying on generated-image lettering.

## Claim boundaries

- **Bring customers back** expresses the intended use case, not an achieved or guaranteed retention result. Do not add revenue, conversion or customer-count evidence that does not exist.
- Branded sender IDs are an incumbent capability claim; do not imply instant approval or that creating an account immediately enables a chosen sender name.
- Omit all prices, discounts, free-account/free-SMS promises and no-expiry language. The owner has confirmed package prices and separate extra-SMS rates; this creative contains no prices. Credit validity is stated as three months.
- Avoid guaranteed delivery, superiority to competitors, unlimited usage, 24/7 support, certifications, customer logos and testimonials without substantiation.
- The recipient UI is an illustrative message, not a verified live portal demonstration. Generated people/business scenes are original creative, not actual customers.


## Price-led copy for a future high-volume variant

Use for agencies and businesses considering large-volume sending, with the package commitment beside the rate. This is copy for a separate creative test; the existing videos remain unchanged.

**Headline:** Extra SMS at LKR 0.45 with SB150X

**Primary text:** Sending at volume? SB150X includes 150,000 SMS for LKR 87,000, with extra SMS at LKR 0.45 each. Credits valid for 3 months. Create your SMS Buddy account.

**Retargeting headline:** Our lowest extra-SMS rate.

Keep “per extra SMS with SB150X” next to every large 0.45 figure and show the package price and included count in the same frame. Do not shorten it to “SMS from 0.45” or claim the lowest rate across Sri Lanka. Compare completed account acquisition before adopting the price-led creative broadly.
