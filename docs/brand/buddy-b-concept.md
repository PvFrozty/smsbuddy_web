## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a refined, exceptionally well-crafted logo concept presentation for "SMS Buddy", a Sri Lankan business SMS platform serving small businesses, agencies and developers. The identity should feel approachable, trustworthy, simple and commercially credible.

Design ONE coherent original identity called Buddy B: a distinctive compact bold geometric letter B fused with the silhouette of a speech bubble. Two generous rounded lobes subtly suggest two messages/buddies, with clean negative space and a single intentional speech tail. Clever but instantly legible. Use few shapes, optical corrections and consistent geometry. No eyes, no smile face, no robot, no dots, no envelope, no paper plane, no lightning, no extra symbols. It must read beautifully at favicon scale and work as a single flat color. Avoid an ordinary chat bubble with a B merely printed inside: the B IS the bubble silhouette.

Color: vivid cobalt #244CEB symbol, deep navy #12234B wordmark, white #FFFFFF background, very pale blue #EDF3FF only for a secondary panel. Typography: bespoke-looking bold friendly geometric sans in the spirit of Manrope, exact text "SMS Buddy", immaculate optical kerning, capital SMS then capital B and lowercase uddy. No slogan. Flat vector-style geometry, razor-sharp solid fills, no gradients, no shadow, no 3D, no texture, no photography.

Compose one premium landscape identity presentation sheet, around 3:2 aspect. Upper two-thirds: generous empty white space surrounding one large primary horizontal lockup, symbol left and "SMS Buddy" wordmark right. Bottom third: three restrained equally sized applications separated by whitespace: standalone cobalt symbol on white; reversed white horizontal lockup on a solid cobalt rectangular field; compact monochrome navy symbol on pale blue. Repeat EXACT same master geometry across all applications, no alternate designs. Tiny editorial text "BUDDY B" may appear at top left and "SMS Buddy / Identity concept" at bottom left, no other explanatory copy. Crisp polished design studio presentation. Prioritize uniqueness, balanced spacing and excellent visual clarity.
